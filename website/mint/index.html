Understand Mystery mint risk

Mystery uses on-chain block-derived randomness, not Chainlink VRF. It is not cryptographically manipulation-resistant. Each pass is settled from the hash of the Ethereum block after its mint block, so the proposer of that block can bias the result, and validators and block builders can influence transaction inclusion and ordering. Because the burn is final before that hash exists, buyers and smart-contract buyers cannot preview, pick or revert an outcome; a reverted reveal changes nothing.

Reveals are open to anyone and happen in mint order; this page sends one for you after your mint. The reveal hash stays readable on-chain for about 27 hours (8,191 blocks). If no one reveals a pass in that time it settles as a Standard pass, so delaying a reveal never helps. Unique outcomes without replacement only ensures that artwork is not assigned twice. This is not guaranteed fairness or tamper-proof randomness. BMC burns are permanent; no refunds are provided by the NFT contract.

How the burn works

  • The NFT contract calls BMC burnFrom and verifies that both your balance and BMC total supply drop by exactly the mint price. BMC is never transferred to a developer wallet.
  • If allowance is insufficient, this page requests approval for the exact single-mint price. It never requests unlimited approval. An approval alone does not mint or burn.
  • The phase or supply can change while transactions are pending. If minting reverts, its burn rolls back; gas may still be spent and a previous approval may remain. Check any pending transaction before trying again.
  • Genesis artwork and Standard metadata are fixed. The owner's only power is to open minting once after deployment, verification and authorization; there is no pause, close, special mint, price-change, withdrawal or upgrade power. A PASS is a collectible; this page promises no financial return.
