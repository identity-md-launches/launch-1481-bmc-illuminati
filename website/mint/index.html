Understand the burn and reveal

BMC burns are permanent. The contract calls burnFrom and verifies that your balance and total token supply both decrease by the exact price. No BMC is paid to a developer wallet. A failed mint rolls back the burn, but spent gas and a prior approval may remain.

Mystery randomness and expiry

Mystery uses the hash of the block after minting, not Chainlink VRF. It is not cryptographically manipulation-resistant: block proposers can bias outcomes and builders can influence ordering. The burn happens before the outcome exists.

Anyone may reveal pending Passes in mint order. Press “Reveal pending passes” after the next block is available and review the transaction. After the 8,191-block history window (roughly 27 hours at 12 seconds per block), reveal selects a remaining Standard outcome if available; otherwise it selects from the remaining Genesis inventory. This is not guaranteed fairness or tamper-proof randomness.

Permissions and transaction safety

The UI requests only exact-price approval, never unlimited approval. An approval does not mint. Every send requires a separate on-page review and wallet confirmation. Account, network, code, phase and balance are checked again before sending. A pending transaction blocks resubmission; check its hash on Etherscan or in wallet activity.

The reviewed Pass has no privileged mint, price change, withdrawal, pause or upgrade function. The owner's one-way opening power remains separate from this website. Local tests do not replace an independent security review.
